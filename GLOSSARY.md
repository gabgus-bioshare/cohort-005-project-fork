# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around team licences and getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Reviewer**:
The person who decides Refund Requests and confirms whether their Refunds were sent. Only platform admins can be Reviewers.
_Avoid_: Support, the team, Team Admin (the admin of a team licence, a different role)

**Team Admin**:
A person who manages a team's Team Licences on its behalf.
_Avoid_: Admin (that is the platform role), Owner

### Team Licences

**Team Licence**:
A purchase of several Seats in one course, made for a team.
_Avoid_: Bulk purchase, Coupon pack

**Seat**:
One place on a Team Licence, handed out as a code a learner can redeem.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Licence key

**Claimed Seat**:
A Seat a learner has redeemed, so it now belongs to them.

**Unclaimed Seat**:
A Seat nobody has redeemed yet.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. Usually the learner raises it; a Reviewer can also raise one for them. For a Team Licence, a Team Admin raises it and it covers chosen Seats rather than the whole purchase. A Reviewer approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Reviewer has decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Reviewer has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund. It becomes declined if its Refund is cancelled.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Reviewer has turned down, with a Decline Reason, either outright or by cancelling its Refund. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Decline Reason**:
The Reviewer's explanation to the learner of why their Refund Request was declined.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount paid for what it covers: the whole purchase, or, for a Team Licence, each covered Seat's equal share of the licence price. A declined Refund Request has no Refund; an Approved Refund Request can have several Refunds over time if earlier ones failed, but only one that hasn't failed.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund whose Refund Request has been approved, where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. It is final: the Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed, and a Reviewer can start a new Refund for it.

**Failure Note**:
The Reviewer's explanation to the learner of why their Refund couldn't be sent, and what they can do about it.

**Cancelled Refund**:
A Pending or Failed Refund a Reviewer has called off for good, before any money was sent, because it shouldn't or can't go ahead. Its Refund Request becomes declined.
_Avoid_: Reversed, Revoked
