# Course Platform

An online platform where learners buy and take video courses.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's request to get their money back for a purchase. It is Requested until someone approves or declines it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Reversal request, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner when a Refund Request is approved. It also takes away the learner's access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancelled purchase, Return

**Refund Window**:
The 14 days after a purchase during which the learner can make a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancellation window, Cancel window
