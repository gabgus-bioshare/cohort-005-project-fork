# Refunds end access but keep progress

When a Refund is Issued, the learner's enrollment in the course is removed, but their lesson progress, quiz attempts, video watch history and comments are kept. That way a learner who buys the course again picks up where they left off, and nothing they wrote vanishes from discussions. Keeping it costs little: every view that shows progress already goes through enrollments, so an unenrolled learner's leftover progress stays hidden.

## Consequences

- Refund eligibility totals all of a learner's watch time in a course, including time from before an earlier Refund. A learner who is refunded, buys again and asks for another refund carries their old watch time with them. This is intended, because it blocks a watch, refund, re-buy cycle.
- The progress of learners who never come back is never cleaned up.
