# Team Licence refunds are per Seat

A Team Licence is one purchase covering many Seats, and some of them may already have been claimed and watched beyond refund eligibility. Refusing the whole licence because one Seat is claimed (the original rule) left teams unable to get anything back. Refunding the whole licence regardless gave away content that had been used. So a Refund Request on a Team Licence covers specific Seats chosen by a Team Admin, and its Refund is the sum of those Seats' equal shares of the licence price. A Seat qualifies if it is unclaimed, or if its holder has watched under two hours. The 14-day window is counted from the licence purchase for every Seat.

## Consequences

- "A Refund is always the full amount paid" now means the full amount for what the Refund covers, not for the whole purchase.
- One Team Licence can have several Refund Requests over time (still only one open at a time). Each Seat can be in at most one Refund Request that hasn't been declined.
- Seats in an undecided Refund Request, or with a Pending Refund, cannot be redeemed. Once the Refund is Issued, they are gone for good, and claimed holders lose access.
- The Refund goes to whoever paid for the licence, even though any Team Admin may request it.
